Experience

Postdoctoral Appointee

Argonne National Laboratory
Jan 2025 – Present

At Argonne, I develop hydrocarbon-based membranes. I helped specify and procure a GPC system, introduced GPC monitoring to track reaction progress, and developed procedures that improved batch-to-batch reproducibility. I evaluate conductivity across batches using EIS, support industrial sample qualification, and help establish membrane-synthesis workflows. I conduct SAXS/GISAXS measurements with beamline scientists.

PhD in Chemistry

Michigan State University
2018–2024

At Michigan State, I investigated ruthenium-catalyzed ammonia oxidation, using NMR and Raman spectroscopy to characterize intermediates and stoichiometric product analysis to support a revised mechanism involving a second pathway (JACS). In separate work, I used 15N NMR to track ammonia conversion to N2 by a coordinatively saturated Ru–Cl catalyst (Inorganic Chemistry). I also trained junior researchers and served as a teaching assistant.

Organic Chemist, Intern

KLA
Summer 2024

At KLA, I synthesized rhodamine-based photoswitchable dyes for inspection technology and characterized their optical properties using UV–Vis and fluorescence spectroscopy.
