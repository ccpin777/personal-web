Research direction

Research Focus

Catalysis, membranes, and energy materials

My work combines synthesis, spectroscopy, and electrochemistry to understand how molecular structure affects material performance in catalysis, membranes, and energy applications. My experience includes variable-temperature and multinuclear NMR, isotope-labeling experiments, ionic-conductivity measurements, and ion-permselectivity measurements.
