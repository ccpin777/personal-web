01 / The work

From a molecule
to a working material.

The questions have changed over time, but the habit has stayed the same: start with what the material is doing, then work backward to find out why.

NOW

Polymer membranes

At Argonne, I study how functional groups and polymer composition affect proton conductivity in hydrocarbon-based membranes for fuel cells.

2018-23

Ammonia oxidation

At Michigan State, I used multinuclear NMR, Raman spectroscopy, and synthesis to understand electrocatalytic ammonia oxidation.

2024

Industry perspective

At KLA, I adapted organic synthesis and optical characterization for rhodamine-based photoswitchable dyes.

The thread connecting these projects is structure-property thinking: using evidence to explain performance, not just report it.
